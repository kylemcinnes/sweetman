About Tim

Local knowledge, plainly delivered.

Tim Sweetman is the broker at Sweetman Real Estate in Plantation. He has spent more than 20 years helping people buy and sell homes in South Florida, with his work centered on Broward County.

His approach is straightforward. He studies what the market is actually doing, says what a home is worth, and looks for the property that fits the way a client lives. Buyers get a focused search. Sellers get a price they can explain. Everyone gets a direct line to Tim.

Clients on Zillow have given him a 5.0 rating across 8 reviews. That profile also shows 100 recorded sales, from more modest homes through higher-priced properties. If you want an accurate look at a neighborhood or a specific house, start there — or call him.

Where he works Plantation, Davie, Fort Lauderdale, Weston, Sunrise, Cooper City, Southwest Ranches, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Tamarac, Coral Springs, and Parkland.
